Nevertheless, there are encouraging data on the prevention of chronic disease through nutrition, notably obesity and cardiovascular disease. It has been estimated, for example, that the prevention of age-related rise in blood cholesterol by 1 mmol/l would correspond, in 45 year- old men, to roughly 40% fewer deaths, and 20% before age 60 (Westlund 1998).
