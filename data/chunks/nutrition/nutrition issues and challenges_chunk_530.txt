HIV/AIDS is a dramatic public health problem in most of the developing world. Malnutrition is common in HIV infection, and indeed it was one of the earliest identified complications of AIDS. The principal nutritional issues in HIV infection are the interrelationships between nutritional status and the progression of the disease, and the risk of vertical transmission of HIV through breastfeeding.
