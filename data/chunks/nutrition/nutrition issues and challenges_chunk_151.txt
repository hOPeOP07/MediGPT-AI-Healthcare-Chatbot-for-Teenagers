In Japanese boys of short stature with marginal zinc deficiency based on a zinc clearance test, zinc supplements improved the height velocity, but this was not observed in girls (Kaji et al, 1998). In Chile also, zinc supplements increased linear growth of stunted pre-adolescent and adolescent males, but not females, in a randomized placebo-controlled trial (Castillo-Duran et al, 1994).
