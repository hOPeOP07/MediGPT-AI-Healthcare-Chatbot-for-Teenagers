Furthermore, the deleterious effects of deficiencies in adolescents, and of nutrition-related disorders such as obesity, are ignored in these figures, in the short and in the long term (calcium, for instance, with osteoporosis), possibly owing to as yet insufficient data on their contribution to morbidity and mortality.
