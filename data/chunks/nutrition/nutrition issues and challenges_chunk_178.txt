According to NHANES III (Hickman et al, 1998) as in previous surveys in the USA, it was found that mean total cholesterol was higher in female than male adolescents, and that the highest levels were found in 9 to 11-year olds. There was a declining trend, as in adults, from previous studies among all 12 to 17 year-old adolescent race/sex groups (Mexican-Americans, non-hispanic whites), except in black females, who had the highest total serum cholesterol levels, and who experienced the smallest decrease over time.
