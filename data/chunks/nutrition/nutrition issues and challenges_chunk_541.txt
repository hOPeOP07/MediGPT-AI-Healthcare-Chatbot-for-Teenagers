HIV-infected women should be advised to breastfeed or not has been an issue since the transmission of HIV by breast milk was observed in the mid 80s. Safe alternatives to breastfeeding should be considered, based on a mother’s right to choose the best methods for feeding her child, according to the United Nations’ policy of 1997. This policy is accompanied by a set of guidelines for health-care managers and policy-makers.
