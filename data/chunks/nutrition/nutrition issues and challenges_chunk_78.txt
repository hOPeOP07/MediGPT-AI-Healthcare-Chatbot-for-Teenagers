Inadequate nutrition in adolescence can potentially retard growth and sexual maturation, although these are likely consequences of chronic malnutrition in early infancy and childhood. It can affect adolescents’ current health and put them at high risk of chronic disease as well, particularly if combined with other adverse lifestyle patterns, even if the detrimental effects may take long to show. Compounded with growth, adolescent pregnancy exposes both mother and child to adverse health and socioeconomic consequences, particularly if the mother is stunted or undernourished.
