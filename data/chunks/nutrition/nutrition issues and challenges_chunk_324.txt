These programmes and services include: effective education in food and nutrition; a school environment that provides opportunity and reinforcement for healthy eating and physical activity; involvement of parents and the community; and screening, counselling and referral for nutrition problems as part of school health services. Teaching methods based on the social learning theory have been advocated for school-based nutrition education.
