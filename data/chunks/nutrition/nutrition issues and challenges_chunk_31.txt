There is an important interaction between economic opportunity and attitudes of the youth adolescents may have little incentive to invest in education and to stay away from health-threatening activities or behaviours. Conversely, without the prospect of a qualified, healthy and productive workforce, potential investors may be reluctant to commit to economic development. There are substantial costs to governments, societies and individuals for every failure of youth to reach adulthood alive, healthy, well educated and without dependants for whom they cannot care.
