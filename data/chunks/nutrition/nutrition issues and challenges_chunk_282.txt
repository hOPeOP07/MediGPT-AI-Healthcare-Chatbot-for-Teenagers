The common adolescent health agenda of WHO, UNFPA and UNICEF (WHO 1997a) provides a useful framework for integrated and successful programmes, which should be designed to: 1) provide accurate knowledge; 2) build skills; 3) provide counselling; 4) improve access to health services; and 5) create safe and supportive environments. The challenges, guiding concepts, intervention settings, key players and keys to success are mapped.
