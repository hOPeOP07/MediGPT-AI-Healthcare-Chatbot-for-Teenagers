In other words, beyond biological programming, social patterning effects have to be considered. Adolescence may be a particularly relevant time for social patterning influences. At least in certain population groups, another long-term benefit of improved nutrition in adolescence, particularly in girls, is the reduced risk of osteoporosis in older age.
