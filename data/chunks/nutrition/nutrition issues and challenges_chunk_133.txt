The girls with the lowest retinol level were found to have lower values for Hb and other biochemical indices including serum iron and transferrin saturation, even after adjustments were made for potentially confounding socio- demographic factors. This again suggests that vitamin A deficiency may have contributed to the aetiology of iron deficiency anaemia. Thus, marginal vitamin A status may compromise iron metabolism.
