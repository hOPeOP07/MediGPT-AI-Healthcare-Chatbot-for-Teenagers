Nutrition education, food security and access to nutrition services are then the three major components of nutrition promotion. In accordance with its mandate, the health-care sector is primarily involved in providing nutrition education and services, but health workers have to be aware of food security issues and link with food security programmes. Food security (supply of, and access to enough, nutritious, safe and culturally acceptable food) is a critical environmental determinant of nutritional health.
