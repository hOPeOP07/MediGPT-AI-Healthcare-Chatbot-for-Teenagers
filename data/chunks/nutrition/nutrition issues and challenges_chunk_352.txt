Upstream improvements in food systems may be needed, along with income interventions for the poor (Delisle 1998a). Nutrition can also benefit from improvements in personal and environmental health conditions, meaning that health programmes and health care already make a contribution through disease prevention and control, infections in particular. Furthermore, reproductive health activities may have a substantive impact on nutrition of adolescents and of the next generation, through delaying the first pregnancy, adequate antenatal care, and subsequently spacing of pregnancies.
