In general, longitudinal studies suggest that obesity tracks into adulthood particularly if it is present in adolescence (Serdula et al, 1993). While a genetic component in the aetiology is acknowledged, environmental factors play a central role. High-fat diets and sedentary lifestyles are considered major causes (WHO 1998a).
