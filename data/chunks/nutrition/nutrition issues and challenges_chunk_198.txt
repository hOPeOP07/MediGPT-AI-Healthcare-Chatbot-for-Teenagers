There may be large differences among countries in the proportion of girls bearing a child before the age of 20 (Bongaarts and Cohen, 1998). The proportion may reach two thirds, as in Bangladesh, Cameroon, Mali and Niger, whereas it is below 20% in countries such as Morocco, Sri Lanka and Tunisia.
