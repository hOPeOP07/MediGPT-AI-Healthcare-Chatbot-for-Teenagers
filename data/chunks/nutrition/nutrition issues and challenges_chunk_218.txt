As summarized by PAHO13 before its recent international conference in Brazil on strategies to combat teenage pregnancy, poverty increases the risk of teenage pregnancy: in countries of the region with medium-to-high fertility rates, approximately 70% of pregnant adolescents belong to the most disadvantaged groups, and the rates are even higher in rural areas. Studies in Chili and Mexico indicated that 75% of pregnant adolescents were themselves daughters of adolescent mothers.
