Nutrition intervention among adolescents and as part of antenatal care may also make a substantive contribution to breaking this vicious cycle (see under Section 2.4, and Chapter 4). 3.4 Lifestyle and eating patterns, major threats to adolescents’ nutritional status Nutritional problems of adolescents, whether undernutrition or nutrition-related chronic diseases, are mainly the result of dietary inadequacies, which may be related to a number of factors that can be grouped under physiological, socioeconomic, and psychosocial factors (see Fig. 1). Growth imposes additional nutritional requirements in adolescence, and these requirements are further increased in pregnancy.
