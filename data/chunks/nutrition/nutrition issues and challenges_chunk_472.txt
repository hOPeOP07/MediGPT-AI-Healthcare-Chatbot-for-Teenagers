Nutritional care of adolescent mothers in the postpartum During the postpartum, women, and particularly adolescents, require nutritional attention for breastfeeding support, for dietary advice and for food supplementation in conditions of severe lack of access to food, and for micronutrient supplementation where appropriate (vitamin A, iodine, iron), much the same as during pregnancy (WHO 1998d).
