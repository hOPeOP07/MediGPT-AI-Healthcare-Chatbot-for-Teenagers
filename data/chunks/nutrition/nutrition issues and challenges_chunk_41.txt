Web site: www.paho.org/hpp/hppabout.htm, 16/04/99 Regarding health more specifically, a common agenda was developed by WHO, UNFPA and UNICEF (WHO 1997a; 1999a) regarding actions for the health of adolescents, with the focus on improving knowledge, skills, access to counselling and health services, and safety and support of the environment (see Chapter 4). In ‘Health21: The Health for all Policy Framework for the WHO European Region’, as revised in 1998, one of the two main aims is to promote and protect people’s health throughout their lives, the other one being to reduce the incidence of the main diseases and injuries, and to alleviate the suffering they cause.
