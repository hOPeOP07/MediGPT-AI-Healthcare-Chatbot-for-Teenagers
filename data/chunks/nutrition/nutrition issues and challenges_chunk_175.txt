However, anthropometric indices of fat distribution may vary across population groups of adolescents as they do in adults (Karter et al, 1996) and therefore, additional studies in adolescents are required. Waist-hip ratios are also difficult to standardize in children because of changes in body habitus with normal development (Gidding et al, 1996).
