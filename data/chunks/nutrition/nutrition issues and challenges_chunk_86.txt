While the first three are nutritional problems as such, the last two are risk factors that may result in nutritional problems. No ranking is attempted because while they are all critical, there are important variations in their relative magnitude, and hence in priority issues, depending on the population group considered. There are other nutrition-related problems that need not be overlooked, dental health problems for instance.
