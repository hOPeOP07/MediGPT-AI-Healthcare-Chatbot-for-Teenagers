Control of iodine deficiency is imperative, considering the dramatic repercussions on neonatal mortality and mental development, and it is well under way (see Chapter 4). Understandably, women of reproductive age are a priority target group for intervention. Iodine deficiency is assessed by goitre and urinary iodine levels among schoolchildren, as these are captive groups. Some adolescents are ‘captured’ in these surveys, but evaluation or surveillance data are not reported separately for this group.
