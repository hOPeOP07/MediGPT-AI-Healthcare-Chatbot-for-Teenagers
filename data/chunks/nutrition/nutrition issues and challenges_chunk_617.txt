However, there are other plant foods that provide more bioavailable provitamin A, the vegetable matrix may be broken down through cooking or grinding, and the highest vegetable source of provitamin A, that is unrefined palm oil, is devoid of a vegetable matrix. Deworming and infection control are complementary but necessary approaches to improve vitamin A status, in adolescents as well as in other age groups. There is an opportunity to pilot-test among adolescents the integration of vitamin A food production or processing for income generation with educational components.
