Outcomes to consider are maternal mortality and morbidity, birth weights and prematurity, and lactational performance according to age, maturity and environmental factors. Socioeconomic consequences of early pregnancy also have to be addressed. Maternal mortality and morbidity Many studies have shown that adolescent girls have a higher maternal mortality rate than women aged 20-34 (Royston and Armstrong, 1989; Kurz 1997). As observed in Bangladesh, Nigeria and Jamaica, maternal mortality rates are particularly high among the youngest adolescents, that is 10-14 years of age.
