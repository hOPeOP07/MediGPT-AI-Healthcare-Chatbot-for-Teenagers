N U T R I T I O N I N A D O L E S C E N C E High-dosage vitamin A supplements are not recommended during pregnancy because of potential teratogenic effects. However, in areas where maternal vitamin A deficiency is common, notably in Asia, low-dose supplements during pregnancy may be highly beneficial (WHO 1998c).
