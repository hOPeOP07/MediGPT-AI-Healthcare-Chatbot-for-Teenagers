Selected school-based strategies are graded according to age of schoolchildren, and focus on making the food environment more health-enhancing, on enhancing personal characteristics that will support healthy eating, and on enhancing behavioural capabilities that will support healthy eating. The American Dietetic Association (ADA), the Society for Nutrition Education, and the American School Food Service Association jointly issued a position statement in 1995 (ADA Reports). The position is that comprehensive school-based nutrition programmes and services be provided to all the nation’s [USA] elementary and secondary students.
