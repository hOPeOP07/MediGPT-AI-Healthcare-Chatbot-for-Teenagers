Paradoxically, feeding young pregnant lambs well leads to a selective channelling of nutrients to the mother, who then thrives at the expense of the foetus. Obviously, research is needed to verify whether such an adverse effect may occur with food supplementation of While food supplements are shown to be beneficial when given to mothers who were nutritionally compromised, they represent only an interim solution. The underlying causes of women’s malnutrition need to be sought.
