Is antenatal care nutritionally effective? The effectiveness of antenatal care in general, and for adolescents in particular, is questioned in developing countries (McDonagh 1996; Brabin et al, 1998). In a review of health problems and health care in adolescent pregnancy (Treffers 1998), the inadequacies of antenatal care in developing countries, in particular for pregnant adolescents, are also underlined. Antenatal care may be scarce for women of any age. There is a high proportion of adolescents getting no antenatal care, or else coming late in their pregnancy.
