For adolescents, particularly if they cannot be reached through schools, alternative environmental strategies have to be developed. Whilst school-based health programmes are critically important, the school cannot solve all health and social problems (Nutbeam 1997). Schools may produce well informed youth with skills to manage their life and their health, but they may not be able to live accordingly for reasons beyond their control. Empowering young people should not convey the message that the solution of their problems is essentially within themselves.
