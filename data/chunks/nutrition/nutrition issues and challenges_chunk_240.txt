In a participatory study on adolescent knowledge, values and coping strategies in relation to health in urban Kenya (Balmer et al, 1997), emerging sexuality, drugs and alcohol ranked at the top of the list of concerns, and politics, language and leisure and sports at the bottom of the list. It is revealing that apart from STD/AIDS, health (or food) did not emerge as a concern, nor as a value, while parents’ love, education and honesty were highly rated.
