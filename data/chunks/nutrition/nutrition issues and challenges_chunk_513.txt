Oedema from non-nutritional causes needs to be identified by the history and the medical examination. In adults (or in adolescents having gone through the growth spurt two years before), the BMI cut-off point for undernutrition would be 16, but this represents approximately 20% of body-weight deficit, whereas weight deficits of 40% are common among adults in famines.
