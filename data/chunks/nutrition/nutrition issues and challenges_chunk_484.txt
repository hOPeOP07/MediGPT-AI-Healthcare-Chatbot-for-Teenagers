At the onset of the trial, serum lipid profiles and hyperinsulinemia in obese subjects suggested that insulin resistance syndrome (or X Syndrome) may already manifest itself in childhood. After one year of treatment, relative weight decreased by 16%, serum lipid profiles had improved, and there was a modest decrease in fasting serum insulin. The changes remained stable during the next year. After five years, many changes had reversed, but HDL-cholesterol remained elevated, and the average weight loss was 13% at this point.
