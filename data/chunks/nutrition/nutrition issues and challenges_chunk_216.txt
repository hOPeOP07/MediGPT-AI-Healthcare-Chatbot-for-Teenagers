However, it is important to compare with adult childbearers in order to control for confounding factors, and to study cases over a long enough period in order to verify whether the disadvantages are only transitory or long-lasting. In the ICRW studies (Mexico, Chile, Barbados and Guatemala), it was found that early childbearing had more marked economic than social effects, and for poor rather than for all mothers.
