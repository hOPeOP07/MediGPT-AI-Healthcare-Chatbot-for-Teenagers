Neuromotor and cognitive impairments of variable degrees are the most important consequences of iodine deficiency (Stanbury 1998). Iodine deficiency is recognized as the most common cause of preventable mental retardation in the world. Cretinism is only the extreme end of the spectrum. In iodine-deficiency areas, a downward shift of IQs has been reported (Bleichrodt et al, 1989), as well as lower performance in school after controlling for confounding variables (Fierro-Benitez et al, 1986).
