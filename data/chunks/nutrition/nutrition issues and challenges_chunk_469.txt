This suggests that the maximum benefit to be expected will be achieved with early initiation of the supplementation. However, there may be some concern with food supplementation of adolescent pregnant women, as it has been suggested that it may exacerbate the risk of intrauterine growth retardation, based on observations in animals (James 1997; Wallace et al, 1996, 1997). While in adult pregnancy the hormonal response is normally geared to optimizing the flow of nutrients to the foetus, the opposite seems to occur in adolescent pregnancies.
