Growth of pelvic bones continues for another 2-3 years after height growth has stopped (Moerman 1982). Maximum bone mass is not achieved before 25 years (National Academy of Sciences 1997). In undernourished populations, growth rate during adolescence is slower (Eveleth and Tanner, 1990). Using maximum growth spurt or menarche as an indicator, maturation may be delayed in malnourished girls by an average of two years (Dreizen, Spirakis and Stone, 1967).
