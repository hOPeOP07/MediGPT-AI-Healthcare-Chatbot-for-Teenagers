The declining trend in serum cholesterol accompanies a decreasing level of total and saturated fat intake as % of total calories, and declining cholesterol intake, although intake levels are still too high. Overweight is also highly prevalent, and adolescents show a low level of physical activity. However, total serum cholesterol does not distinguish between “bad” and “good” cholesterol, which fluctuates during puberty. For instance, the decline of total cholesterol at puberty in males reflects the decrease of HDL-cholesterol.
