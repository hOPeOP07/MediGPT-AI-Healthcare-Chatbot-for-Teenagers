Poor glycemic control was associated with poorer growth and higher total cholesterol levels during a five-year follow- up of children with type 1 diabetes (Gunczler et al, 1996). A consistent observation is that adolescents from more supportive and cohesive families have a better metabolic control (Burrough et al, 1997). In a study in the USA, it was observed that fostering parent-adolescent teamwork in routine follow-up visits of adolescents with type 1 diabetes sustained shared responsibility for insulin administration and glucose monitoring without increasing diabetes-related family conflicts (Anderson et al, 1999).
