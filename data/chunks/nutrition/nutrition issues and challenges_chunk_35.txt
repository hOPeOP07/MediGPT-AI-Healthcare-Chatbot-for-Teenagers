The costs and effects develop over a lifetime (Burt 1996). Reproductive health is understandably a major concern, with the human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), other sexually transmitted diseases (STD) and early pregnancy as main issues. One third of new STD cases, more than half the new HIV infections and one third of all births are among youth.
