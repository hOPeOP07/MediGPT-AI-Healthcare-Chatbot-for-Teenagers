Conversely, adolescent girls have a higher self and body awareness than boys, and they tend to be less satisfied not only with their body, but also their appearance, their health, their personality (Cordonnier 1995). While young girls from western nations are more exposed to problems such as eating disorders, young women from developing countries are apparently at higher risk for suicide. A majority of adolescents think they are in good health, and they tend to feel invulnerable, with little motivation to protect their health “capital” for the future.
