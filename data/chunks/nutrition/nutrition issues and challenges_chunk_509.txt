Arm circumference is another potentially useful indicator in adolescents, but it has not been validated and no cut-off values exist for adolescents. Discharge criteria are also seriously lacking. Much operational research is needed on these issues. Appropriate attention to adolescents’ and adults’ malnutrition in severe famine is vitally important since they are the most economically productive groups, and since rehabilitation is often successful with appropriate care. Many of these people could be saved with some redirection of resources towards adult therapeutic units (Collins 1993).
