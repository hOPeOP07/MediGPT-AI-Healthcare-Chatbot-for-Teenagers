There is a dire need for more research in ‘public’ nutrition in general, and for research focusing on adolescents in particular. There is a great need not only for research, but also for dissemination of research findings, as well as programme evaluation. Success stories are not many in the published material on those for nutrition, and yet, they are important as a means of spreading best practices in the field. Intervention rather than observational studies are now needed for progress in effectively addressing nutrition-related needs of adolescents.
