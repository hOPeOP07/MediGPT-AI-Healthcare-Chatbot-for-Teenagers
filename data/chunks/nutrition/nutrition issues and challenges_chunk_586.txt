If asked whether enough is done for adolescent nutrition, a legitimate answer could be: we could do better. It is concluded that adolescents are a nutritionally vulnerable group in many ways (perhaps the third, behind pre-schoolers and mothers), and that unless there are explicit policies and priorities for adolescent nutrition at country level, key issues may not be adequately addressed, and in particular, large groups of adolescents may be missed.
