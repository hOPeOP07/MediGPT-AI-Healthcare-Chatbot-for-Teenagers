Body image is important in adolescence, and disturbances are in relation with obesity, dietary disorders and psychological discontent. Based on available evidence, concerns with body image exist across gender, BMI and ethnic groups. Dissatisfaction with body weight and shape is widespread, probably more so in Caucasian adolescents, and it is not only associated with overweight. These issues need to be addressed by all those who work with youth.
