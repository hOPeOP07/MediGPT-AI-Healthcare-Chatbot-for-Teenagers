More evidence is still needed on the effectiveness of various food-based vitamin A programmes, including gardening, food processing, commercial marketing and food-to-food fortification schemes. Periodic high-dosage vitamin A supplementation in school-age children in addition to under-fives (and one dose post-partum in women) is currently under consideration in areas where vitamin A deficiency is widespread.
