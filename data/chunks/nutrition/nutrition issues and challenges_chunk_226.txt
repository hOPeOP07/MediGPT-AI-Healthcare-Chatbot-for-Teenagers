These food habits may also apply in varying degrees to adolescents in other parts of the world. Other eating behaviours generally recognized as common among adolescents are eating away from home, low intake of fruits and vegetables, and in some instances, of dairy products as well; and weight concerns leading to faulty dieting practices, particularly in girls (Forthing 1991; Perry-Hummicuft and Newman, 1993; Gutierrez and King, 1993).
