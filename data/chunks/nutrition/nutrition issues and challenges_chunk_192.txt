Tooth decay, a nutrition-related problem in adolescence Tooth decay and periodontal disease may result from a combination of poor oral hygiene and poor eating habits. At this time, dental problems are more widespread in industrialized than developing countries. Education for dental hygiene and health and fluoridation of drinking water and toothpastes, and to a certain extent the use of sugar substitutes, are contributing to a reduction in numbers of cavities, filled and missing teeth.
