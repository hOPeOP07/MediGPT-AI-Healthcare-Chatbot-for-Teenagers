Obesity represents a major burden on health of individuals, and on health-care resources. In Canada, for instance, based on the national population health survey of 1994, it was found that obese respondents (BMI of 27 or greater) were more likely than non-obese respondents to suffer from stress, activity restrictions, and a number of chronic co-morbidities, including coronary heart disease, stroke, hypertension, diabetes, respiratory, gastrointestinal, and joint problems (Trakas et al, 1999).
