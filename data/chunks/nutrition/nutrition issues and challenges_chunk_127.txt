Translated in economic terms, the overall productivity loss associated with iron deficiency, including cognitive deficits in children, is about US$4 per capita (Ross and Horton, 1998). Iron deficiency has also been shown to reduce endurance among athletes (Raunklar and Sabio, 1992). There is evidence of short-term effects of anaemia on performance capacity and recovery from physical activity, as assessed by heart rate, in British adolescent girls from different ethnic backgrounds (Nelson, Bakaliou and Trivedi, 1994). If even mild anaemia affects physical activity, it may in the long term affect bone and heart health (Nelson 1996), since it may prevent a healthy pattern of physical activity
