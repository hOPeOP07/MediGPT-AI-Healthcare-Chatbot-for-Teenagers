However, more evaluation data on the effects of worksite nutrition programmes in developing countries are needed (Stuart and Achterberg, 1998). 4.3 Overall strategy for nutrition intervention in adolescence Nutrition is at the interface of health, agriculture and education15 , and therefore nutritional health, whether in adolescents or in other age groups, usually requires concerted action on several fronts, not only in health. For instance, and this applies in developing as well as developed countries, adequate access to food is a critical determinant of adequate intake and nutritional status, but it lies by and large outside the health domain. Upstream improvements in food systems may be needed, along with income interventions for the poor
