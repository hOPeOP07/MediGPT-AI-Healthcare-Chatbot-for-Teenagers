Nevertheless, since osteoporosis is relatively less important in developing countries and in non-Caucasian people (Scrimgeour 1992; Bertron, Barnard and Mills, 1999), there is little evidence that dairy products have an effect on osteoporosis in these population groups, and therefore, calcium supplementation would not be justified. Lactose intolerance may be secondary to intestinal brush border alterations in association with infection of malnutrition.
