Controlling parasitic diseases such as malaria, intestinal helminth, and schistosomiasis will also improve iron status. However, all these approaches will not suffice in most instances to protect women against iron deficiency anaemia in view of their particularly high requirements and of limited economic access to animal foods providing highly bioavailable iron, and supplementation is needed. Waiting at the time of pregnancy to correct anaemia in women is too late, and a priority intervention should be the control of iron deficiency through dietary measures and appropriate fortification or supplementation schemes in adolescent girls.
