Inadequate intakes of calcium may be one factor of osteoporosis in older age. Low consumption of dairy products, notably in adolescence, has been associated in developed countries with lower mineral bone mass, and increased osteoporosis risk in adulthood (see Chap. 3). Recommended intakes of calcium have been increased in the USA (National Academy of Sciences 1998), and Canada is also considering doing so (Murray 1996).
