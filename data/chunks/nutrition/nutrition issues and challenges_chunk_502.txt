The Dutch Eating Behavior Questionnaire (DEBQ) is another tool to assess eating disorders (van Strien et al, 1986). The eating disease inventory (EDI) includes three subscales assessing attitudes and behaviours related to eating and body shape: drive for thinness, bulimia and body dissatisfaction, and five subscales assessing general psychological traits relevant to eating disorders (ineffectiveness, perfection, interpersonal distruct, interoceptive awareness, maturity fears).
