It is suggested that the mechanism by which undernutrition delays or suppresses activity of the reproductive axis in adolescents or adult women, is through decreased available energy, rather than body composition changes (Cameron 1996), as suggested by rapid reversal of exercise-induced reproductive dysfunction by increasing food intake, without long-term negative effect on reproductive capacity. Among girls, the “growth spurt” normally takes place between 12 and 18 months before the onset of menarche, which occurs between the ages of 10 and 14.
