Accordingly, review of many case-control studies provides supportive evidence of the hypothesis that wholegrain intake protects against many types of cancer (Jacobs et al, 1998). However, there is insufficient evidence to conclude on a role for sugar (Burley 1998). In the USA, national recommendations are to decrease intake of dietary fat and alcohol, and increase intake of fruits, vegetables and wholegrain cereals. There have been encouraging trends towards improvement consistent with reduced mortality from those cancers with the strongest association with diet: colon/rectum, breast, prostate (Krebs-Smith 1998).
