So maternal stunting is a factor of increased obstetric risk, and it can be attributed to chronic malnutrition, at least in part. In addition, delayed growth and maturation in girls as a result of malnutrition further increases the risks associated with adolescent pregnancy, as biological age lags behind chronological age (see under Section 3.3).
