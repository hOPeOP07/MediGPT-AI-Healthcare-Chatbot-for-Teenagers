Interrelationships of eating and other health-related lifestyle factors Studies show that healthy eating and healthy lifestyle in general are frequently associated (Neuwmark- Sztainer et al, 1997). Conversely, adverse behaviours such as drinking, smoking, lack of physical activity, overeating and poor dietary choices tend to cluster together (Woodward, Bolton-Smith and Tunstall- Pedoe, 1994).
