Nonetheless, outside of pregnancy (see below), our view is that nutrient supplementation should be regarded as the exception rather than the rule, considering that globalization of a typically western trend, that is, a pharmaceutical approach to nutrition, is not warranted because of questionable sustainability, effectiveness and even efficacy (Delisle, 1994).
