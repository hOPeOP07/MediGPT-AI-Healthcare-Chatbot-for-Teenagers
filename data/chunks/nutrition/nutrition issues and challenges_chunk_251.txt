For instance, girls were more concerned than boys with their weight, and more felt guilt about eating; while girls showed more health concerns in choosing their food, there were more girls than boys who would sometimes eat out of boredom. It was also found that body image, body weight, and food-associated beliefs and behaviours of 12 to 15-year-old students who had attempted weight loss were significantly different from those of students who had not (Nowak 1998).
