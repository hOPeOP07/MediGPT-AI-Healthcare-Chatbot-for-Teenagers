It would therefore appear that a major change to be encouraged during adolescence and youth is increasing physical activity levels. No long-term effect of diet could be detected, but only fat, carbohydrate and protein were considered. These findings do not rule out possible long-term impact of adolescent lifestyle, for size of sample was small, lifestyle values were averaged over the many measurements, and diet-related assessments were rather crude.
