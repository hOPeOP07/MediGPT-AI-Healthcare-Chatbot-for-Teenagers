Foetal malnutrition as an additional risk factor has been discussed above. It is estimated that half of cardiovascular disease mortality is nutrition-related, as well as 33% to 50% of type-2 diabetes cases (WHO 1990). In a 14-year longitudinal study of over 6000 obese subjects in Germany, it was found that in all age groups, there was an increased mortality risk with obesity, and the risk increased with the level of obesity as assessed on the basis of BMI (Bender et al, 1999).
