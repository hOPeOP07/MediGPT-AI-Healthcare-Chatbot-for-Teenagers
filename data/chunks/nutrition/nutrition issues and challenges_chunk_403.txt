It can be suggested that adolescents are a key target group for inclusive approaches that combine environmental sanitation, hygiene, and regarding food, consumption of vitamin C-rich foods, which enhance iron absorption (Hunt et al, 1994), avoidance of absorption inhibitors such as tea at meal time, emphasis on processes such as fermentation and germination as additional means of increasing iron bioavailability, and finally, increasing as much as possible the intake of animal sources of iron.
