Nutritional aspects of HIV in general, and not specifically for adolescents or developing countries, have been reviewed (MacDougall 1997; Friis and Michaelsen, 1998). Unless otherwise stated, the following data are taken from these reviews. The multifactorial nature of wasting in HIV is well-established, but reduced food intake associated with anorexia, in particular during and malabsorption. Resting energy expenditure is increased by 10% in HIV-infected subjects.
