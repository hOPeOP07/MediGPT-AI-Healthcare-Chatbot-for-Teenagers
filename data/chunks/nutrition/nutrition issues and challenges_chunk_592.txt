Primary focus on nutrition promotion, the key role of schools, and the importance The health-promotion approach allows the integration of all determinants of health and aims at empowering people, two features that make it particularly appropriate for addressing nutrition in adolescents. Forms of action recommended in health promotion are building public-health policy, creating supportive environment, strengthening community action, developing personal skills for healthy lifestyles and reorienting health services.
