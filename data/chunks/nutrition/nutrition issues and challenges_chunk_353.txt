Regarding education, it is now established that an effective strategy for delaying the first pregnancy is to keep girls in school, aside from the positive and strong relationship between women’s education level and the health and nutrition of their children (Leslie 1995). While recognizing the need for a multisectoral approach to adolescents’ nutrition, the strategy and specific interventions discussed below are primarily directed at health-care providers. Community-based food security initiatives, for instance, are not discussed, in spite of their high relevance for nutritional improvement and for adolescents’ involvement, as health is seldom the lead sector.
