Athletes who need to maintain a certain weight for competition may resort to extreme weight-loss measures that can impair both performance and health (diet pills, laxatives, starvation, etc). Weight and dieting concerns of adolescent athletes place them at greater risk of eating disorders (Anderson et al, 1995), and low-energy diets are more likely to be inadequate in micronutrients such as calcium, iron, magnesium, zinc and vitamin B6 (Loosli and Benson, 1990). There may be in adolescents extreme commitment to diet and exercise.
