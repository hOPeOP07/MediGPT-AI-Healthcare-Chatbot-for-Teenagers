Body image, obesity, dieting and eating disturbances Cultural values, and in particular, cultural aspects of body-size perception, may be important in explaining attitudes and practices relative to obesity, as discussed below. Body image, and its disturbances, is a critical determinant of dietary practices and nutritional risk at adolescence, particularly among girls. The concept of body image The concept of ‘body image’ has become nearly synonymous with the physical appearance-related aspect of the notion, and it is currently defined as made of three components: perception of body size and its accuracy; a subjective component of feeling satisfied or not with one’s body; and a behavioural aspect (Heinberg, Wood and Thompson, 1996).
