These factors are a cause for concern as they underlie the lack of effectiveness of iron supplementation, in spite of demonstrated efficacy in well-controlled trials (Yip 1996). Several recommendations have been made to improve effectiveness of iron supplementation programmes, including the use of village-based birth attendants and community-based organizations for distribution (Stoltzfus and Dreyfuss, 1998). Schools and youth groups are among the community-based structures that could be used for delivery of iron (and folate) supplements to adolescent girls, in addition to village health workers.
