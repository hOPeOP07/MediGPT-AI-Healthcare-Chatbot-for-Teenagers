Dietary guidelines have been in existence for a long time in many developed countries, and are periodically updated. When they were evaluated, they proved effective in reaching the target population with relevant advice for healthy eating. More evaluation work is required, with a focus both on the process of developing and implementing the guidelines, and on its impact. Culturally-relevant sets of guidelines may be developed for specific groups within the population, as is being done, for instance for some Amerindian groups in Canada (Mercille and Delisle, 1999).
