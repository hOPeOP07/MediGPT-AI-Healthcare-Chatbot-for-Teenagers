It is interesting that the 1996 study on 25,000 middle-class high-school students aged 15-18 years on five continents found them to be more similar than different in their values and concerns. A gender gap seems to exist irrespective of the setting, in that boys express greater self-confidence, less vulnerability, and more happiness, pride and a subjective sense of well-being than girls.
