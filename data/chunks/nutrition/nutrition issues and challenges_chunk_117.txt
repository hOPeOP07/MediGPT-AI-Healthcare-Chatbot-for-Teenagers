Results showed that in Africa, Oceania, Latin America and the Caribbean, the prevalence was higher among adolescent boys than girls. In the ICRW/USAID studies (Kurz and Johnson-Welch, 1994), anaemia in adolescents was quite high in Nepal (42%), India (55%) one of the two Guatemalan studies (48%) and Cameroon (33%). It was lower in Ecuador (17%) and Jamaica (16%). In rural Guatemala, it was low: 5%.
