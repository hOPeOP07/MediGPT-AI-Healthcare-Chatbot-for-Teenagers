WHO (1997c) now recommends that in areas where the prevalence of mild-moderate underweight in children is greater than 25% and where parasites are known to be widespread, high priority be given to deworming programmes. The programme may also integrate micronutrient supplementation and nutrition education for food-based interventions to prevent micronutrient malnutrition.
