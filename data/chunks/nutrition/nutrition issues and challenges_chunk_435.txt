A major impediment to food diversification schemes for micronutrient nutrition may be that evidence of their effectiveness is still lacking, partly because such interventions are complex and long-term (Howson, Kennedy and Horwitz, ibid.). However, evidence is slowly accumulating. Evaluation research should also encompass, whenever feasible and appropriate, impact on overall nutrition and other positive outcomes such as income effects.
