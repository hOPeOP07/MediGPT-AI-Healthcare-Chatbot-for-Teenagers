It may be that the adolescent is incapable of producing a full complement of milk comparable to that of the adult because of developmental immaturity of the growth process relative to her reproductive capacity (Scholl et al, 1993). There is potential competition between the metabolic needs of the mother for her own maturation and those for milk production. Indeed, it was observed that lean body deposition occurs in adolescents concurrently with milk production, so that there is a dual metabolic challenge (Motil et al, 1996).
