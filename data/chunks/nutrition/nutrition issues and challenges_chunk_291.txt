Social Cognitive Theory (Bandura 1986), the Health Belief Model, as applied to adolescents’ nutrition education (Contento, Manning and Shannon, 1992), the theory of planned behaviour (Ajzen and Madden, 1991), and the concept of Health Locus of Control (Schlenk and Hart, 1984; Houts and Warland, 1989) are among the most commonly used. According to the health belief model, behavioural change occurs when one is convinced of the risk associated with the present behaviour, of the possibility to overcome the obstacles to behavioural change, and of the resulting improved health or reduced health risk.
