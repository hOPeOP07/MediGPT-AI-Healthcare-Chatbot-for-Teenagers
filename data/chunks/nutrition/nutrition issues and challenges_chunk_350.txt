Health and nutrition programmes at the worksite are one way of reaching adolescents already in the workforce, and this would likely be particularly relevant in cities. Such programmes may also be attractive for employers if they contribute to better productivity. Worksite nutrition programmes may revolve around feeding, for instance improving the nutritional quality of street foods or nutrition promotion in cafeterias, or nutrition education may be a self-standing programme.
