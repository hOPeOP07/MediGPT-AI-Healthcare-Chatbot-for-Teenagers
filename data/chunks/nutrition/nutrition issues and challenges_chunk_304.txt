Our contention is that social marketing or communication with particular emphasis on the media is likely very effective for promoting good nutrition (and other health-related behaviours) among adolescents, provided we have clear and simple pre-tested messages to convey. Adolescents enjoy the media, they are innovative, and they are a preferred target for commercial advertising (see Chapter 3). Among Australian adolescents, for example, television was an important source of information on food and nutrition, along with parents and schools (Nowak and Speare 1996).
