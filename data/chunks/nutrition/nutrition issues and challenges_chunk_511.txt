Guidelines on management of severe malnutrition in children, adolescents and adults has recently been published by WHO (1999c). Basically, treatment of severe malnutrition in adolescents is not considered in this document to be very different from that in younger children, with an initial (or acute) treatment phase with liquid feeds and a rehabilitation phase with progressive integration of traditional solid foods once appetite returns.
