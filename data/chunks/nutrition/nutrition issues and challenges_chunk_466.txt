A controlled supplementation trial also showed that calcium supplementation during pregnancy was associated with significantly lower blood pressure in the offspring without any effect on birth weight (Belizan et al, 1997). However, before a general recommendation can be made, the benefits of calcium supplements in Caucasian and non-Caucasian pregnant adolescents, while controlling for their calcium status, deserve further study.
