Zinc-fortified bread was tested on a small scale in a case-control study of Turkey school-age children with low serum zinc levels. Positive results suggesting satisfactory zinc bioavailability were reported (Kilic et al, 1998). In adolescent monkeys, moderate zinc deficiency as sole dietary inadequacy was also shown to limit skeletal growth and mineralization (Golub et al, 1996). These studies provide further supportive evidence for the involvement of zinc deficiency in stunting, perhaps more so in boys.
