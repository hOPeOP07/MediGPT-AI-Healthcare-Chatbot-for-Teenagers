School gardens may make a substantive contribution to eating patterns and nutrition among schoolchildren, and in families at large. In Ecuador, for instance (Chauliac et al, 1996), school gardens were part of the strategy to introduce more fruit and vegetables into local diets, as a means of food diversification and food security in terms of nutritional quality.
