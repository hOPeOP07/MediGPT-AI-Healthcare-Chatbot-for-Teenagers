In India (West Bengal), it was found that even within the same SES group, early menarche girls were heavier and taller than the late menarche girls (Bharati and Bharati, 1998), which would suggest that even if a longer growth period allows for some catch-up, it does not fully compensate for the pre-existing deficit in stature.
