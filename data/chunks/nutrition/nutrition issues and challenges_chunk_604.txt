Obsession with health and tyranny of health appear to be typical trends of industrialized countries, not only the USA. This risk culture, nurtured by epidemiologists and health promoters, may threaten values such as conviviality, sharing, tolerance, and sociability (Førde, ibid). We strongly believe that health and nutrition promotion among adolescents, who are in many ways in the vanguard of society, should stay away from this cultural trend of moral crusade, particularly in developing countries.
