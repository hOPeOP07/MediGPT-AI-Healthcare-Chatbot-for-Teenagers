Only compliance may be easier with the weekly supplement, because of less frequent side effects. Weekly administration, but under supervision, may ensure efficacy. Iodine deficiency represents a major public health problem in many areas of the world. At this time, most countries have undertaken, or are on the verge of doing so, salt iodization, which has been recognized as the most cost-effective method for the control of iodine deficiency (Alnwick 1998).
