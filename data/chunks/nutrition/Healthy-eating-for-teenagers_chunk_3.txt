Cereals, potatoes, rice and pasta: These are all carbohydrate foods which give slow release energy. Try and choose the low sugar varieties of breakfast cereals such as Weetabix, Shredded Wheat, or Shreddies® and add dried fruit rather than sugar. Choose unrefined higher fibre types of bread and pasta such as wholemeal and granary bread, whole wheat pasta and brown rice. Teenagers should aim to have a carbohydrate source at each meal time plus sometimes as snacks between meals
