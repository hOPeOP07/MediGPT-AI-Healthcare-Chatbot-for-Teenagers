The prevalence was as high in boys as in girls, and it was higher among boys in one study (Ecuador). Because of muscle mass development, boys have high iron requirements, as mentioned earlier, although girls are usually expected to have higher anaemia rates due to onset of menarche. However, as the growth of adolescents slows down, boys’ iron status improves. It is not known whether transient anaemia among adolescent boys has functional consequences.
