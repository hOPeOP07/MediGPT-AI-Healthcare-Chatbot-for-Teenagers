The First World Conference of Ministers responsible for youth was held in Lisbon in August 1998 and the Lisbon Declaration on Youth, Policies and Programmes was adopted. It focuses on national youth policy development and implementation, enhanced participation of youth, action for education, development, peace, health and prevention of drug and substance abuse. The only health commitment directly addressing nutrition consists of improving access to malnutrition-prevention programmes as part of basic health care.
