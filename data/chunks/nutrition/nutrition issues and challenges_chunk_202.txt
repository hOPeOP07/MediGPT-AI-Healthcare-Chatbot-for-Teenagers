This is not as yet fully elucidated, although there is evidence in the USA that except for the least mature, the complications of teenage pregnancy are caused more by psychological and socioeconomic factors than by any physical immaturity (American Dietetic Association Reports 1994). As for morbidity, the effect of first childbirth is not always easy to distinguish from that of young age, for instance, in the case of more frequent pregnancy-induced hypertension in adolescents than adults.
