The ‘Life Events’ approach has been used to examine adolescents’ perception of their well-being, and to assess the importance that they give to physical health problems, relative to other events of their daily life (Cordonnier 1995). It was found that for adolescents, psychosocial dimensions of well-being are much more important than physical aspects. Well-being was perceived as strongly dependent upon external events of daily life.
