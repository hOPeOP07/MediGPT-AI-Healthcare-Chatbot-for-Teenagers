Linear growth may be limited by multiple simultaneous nutrient deficiencies in many populations, which could explain that interventions with specific individual nutrients (vg, vitamin A, iron, zinc) have given conflicting results (Allen, 1994). Overall quality of diets may be reflected in growth, as we observed in preschoolers (Tarini, Bakari and Delisle, 1999).
