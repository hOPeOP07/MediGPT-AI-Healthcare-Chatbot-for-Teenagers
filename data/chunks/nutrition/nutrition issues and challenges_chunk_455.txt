Our own view, which would need to be substantiated by observational or research evidence, is that health-care providers seldom have a clear idea of the kind of relevant and context-specific dietary advice that can be given to pregnant women, adolescent or not, owing to the fact that they often have only a superficial knowledge of dietary patterns of women, combined with the absence of local dietary guidelines.
