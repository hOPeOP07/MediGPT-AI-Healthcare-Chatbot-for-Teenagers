The evaluation compared changes in knowledge, practical skills and behaviour over a school year, compared with control schoolchildren. The school-garden intervention had a positive impact on the children at all three levels. In addition, it associated vegetable-eating with satisfaction of consuming the fruit of one’s labour, which was a source of interest and motivation.
