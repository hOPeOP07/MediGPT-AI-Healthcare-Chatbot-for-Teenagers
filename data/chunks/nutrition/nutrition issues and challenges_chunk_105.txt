Malnutrition delays physical growth and maturation There are wide variations among populations in median age at menarche, which ranges from 12.5 years in high income countries, to 15 and above in poorer countries (Becker 1993). Several factors are likely involved in this variance, and nutritional status is considered to be a major one (Bongaarts and Cohen, 1998). Evidence is of four types. First, a relationship between nutritional intakes and the timing of puberty onset has been observed in many populations, with better nourished girls reaching menarche earlier than undernourished girls.
