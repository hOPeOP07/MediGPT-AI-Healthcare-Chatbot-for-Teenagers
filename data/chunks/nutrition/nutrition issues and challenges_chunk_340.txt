Considering only the net outcome resulting in a short time-span from a specific project component is not only difficult with nutrition promotion or education which is usually multi-pronged, it also underestimates the real outcomes and Schools are usually part of the community, and school health and nutrition initiatives should involve the community. This is the case for the school-snack programme in Indonesia, where snack menus, purchases, and preparation are done at community level, and must make use of locally produced foods (Studdert and Soekirman, 1998).
