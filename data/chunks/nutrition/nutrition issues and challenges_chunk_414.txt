The duration of its effectiveness, which may extend up to one year or more, is influenced by many factors, and was shown to be reduced, among others, by a limited amount of body fat, the presence of intestinal parasites, and consumption of cassava (Furnée 1997). There are other alternatives, a useful and low-cost one being the direct oral administration of a potassium iodide solution.
