Other specific conditions that require nutritional management, usually under specialized supervision, and that are not uncommon in adolescents are, among others, food allergies and intolerances, dyslipoproteinemias, etc. Nutrition interventions or services grouped under promotion, prevention and management, and nutritional treatment of specific conditions, are all part of a global adolescent nutrition strategy. As illustrated (Fig.2), nutrition promotion should receive the most attention, and it should permeate preventive and curative activities alike. This figure is however incomplete, as it does not include policy, advocacy, training, surveillance and research components of the global strategy.
