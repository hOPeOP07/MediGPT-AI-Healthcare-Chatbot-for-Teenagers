The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC 1996) has developed, with partnering agencies, a series of guidelines for school health programmes to promote lifelong healthy eating. The following recommendations are made, which may also apply elsewhere. They also converge with those of the WHO’s Global School Health Initiative: Adopt a coordinated school nutrition policy that promotes healthy eating through classroom lessons and a supportive class environment.
