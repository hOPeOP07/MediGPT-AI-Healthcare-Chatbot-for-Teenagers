A roughly twofold increase in the frequency of overweight or obesity was nonetheless reported over a twenty-year period in American and Japanese schoolchild groups (WHO 1998a). In Europe, the prevalence of adult obesity has reportedly increased by about 10%-40% in the last 10 years, and the most dramatic increase (50% since 1980) has been observed in the UK1. However, there is no specific data on adolescents.
