Developing simple tools for dietary inquiry and for appropriate advice on that basis should be regarded as a priority for improved nutrition intervention during pregnancy. Problem trees and solution trees as used in participatory rural appraisal (Chambers 1994) may prove useful for the purpose. It is recognized that reduction of anaemia is one of the most significant contributions of antenatal care (WHO 1992), and in conjunction with iron/folate supplementation, it was shown that where malaria is highly prevalent, prophylaxis is a successful intervention (Brabin 1991).
