This is particularly important when there is a family history of obesity, diabetes, dyslipoproteinemia, or hypertension. Conditions for which weight loss is recommended include hypertension, diabetes mellitus, sleep apnea, orthopedic abnormalities, and severe psychosocial stress resulting from obesity (Gidding et al, 1995). In management of cases of obese adolescents, modest weight loss, combined with treatment of co-morbidity, is advocated (WHO 1998a).
