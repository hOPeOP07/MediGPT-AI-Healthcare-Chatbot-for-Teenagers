A prospective study carried out in Guatemala provides new information on chronic effects of early nutrition on physical work capacity in adolescents (Haas et al, 1996). The study included an intervention phase of high-energy high-protein supplementation during prenatal life and pre-school years, and a follow-up phase of many years. In the 14-19 year-old cohort males, those having been exposed to early supplementation had a significantly higher oxygen uptake than control subjects at near-exertion levels (VO2max), which provides a measure of physical work capacity.
