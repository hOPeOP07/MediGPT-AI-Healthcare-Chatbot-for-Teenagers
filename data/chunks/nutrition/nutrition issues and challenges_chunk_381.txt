This would achieve two-pronged prevention: undernutrition and obesity. A BMI chart such as used in USA adolescents to detect both underweight and overweight (Elster and Kuznets, 1995) could represent a useful tool elsewhere for weight management and prevention. Including some nutrition indicators on adolescents in nutrition monitoring systems would be a means of pointing to adolescents’ nutritional problems. This deserves feasibility research. In the USA, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) established in 1990 the ‘Youth Risk Behavior Surveillance System’ to help monitor progress towards achieving national health and education objectives.
