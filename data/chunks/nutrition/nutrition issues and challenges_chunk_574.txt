Infections and infestations may impair food intake due to anorexia, accelerate body utilization of nutrients because of hypermetabolism, and entail nutrient losses to parasites or through malabsorption. It is now well-established, for instance, that infections alter vitamin A and iron status, which in turn affect resistance to infection. All these health interventions will have even more impact if linked with appropriate nutritional advice and support, which calls for heightened nutrition awareness amongst health professionals and field workers. Adolescents can be reached with younger children in schools, and with adults, for instance in prenatal care in the case of pregnant girls.
