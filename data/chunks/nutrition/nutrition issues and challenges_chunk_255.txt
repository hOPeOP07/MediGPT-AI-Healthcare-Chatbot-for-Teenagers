Many emotionally healthy adolescents exhibit some signs associated with pathological eating disorders. The unique and striking characteristic of eating disorders is the adolescent’s unrealistic and relentless pursuit of thinness. Among girls, concern and preoccupation with thinness is related to SES, race and ethnicity (Moses, Banilivy and Lifshitz, 1989). White and Hispanic girls tend to perceive themselves as overweight more frequently than do black females (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, 1991).
