The UK, Italy and Sweden curves are adjusted for skewness using the least mean square scores. French BMI reference data appear lower than the other reference sets, possibly owing to secular trends, since the French data are older. In a study of Nigerian adolescent girls, for instance, data were adjusted for maturity status according to WHO recommendations (Brabin et al, 1997).
