Fortified foods could target only certain vulnerable groups, and school-based programmes may be well suited for providing fortified foods to children and adolescents as a means of improving their micronutrient status. For instance, in southern Africa, a randomized, case-control trial was conducted on vitamin- and mineral-fortified biscuits and drinks given as snacks to primary-school children (van Stuijvenberg et al, 1999).
