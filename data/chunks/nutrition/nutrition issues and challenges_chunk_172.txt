The obesity- related increased risk was higher in younger age groups, and declined with age. The highest mortality was observed in the group aged 18-29 years at baseline. In a long-term follow-up study of adolescents (Must et al, 1992), it was found that adolescent boys with a BMI above the 75th centile were at increased risk of death from all causes, from cardiovascular disease, and even from colorectal cancer, and both overweight boys and girls were at increased morbidity risk.
