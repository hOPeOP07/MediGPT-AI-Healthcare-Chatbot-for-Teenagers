According to this rights approach, the health-care provider’s role as counsellor is important and challenging, as there is no standard advice that works for everyone (Lhotska 1998). The ultimate decision is in the hands of mothers (WHO 1998f), but it has to be a well-informed decision, and health workers need to help the woman consider all aspects, alternatives and risks in a compassionate way, for this is a very stressful situation for women.
