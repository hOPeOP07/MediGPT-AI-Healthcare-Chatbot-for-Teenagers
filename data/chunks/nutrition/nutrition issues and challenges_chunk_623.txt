So little is known on nutrition in adolescence that research needs are tremendous. Many lines of investigation have been suggested throughout the document. They are listed below under specific sub-headings. On eating behaviours and determinants in adolescents: A better understanding of adolescents’ diets and eating behaviours is essential for relevant education programmes. Additionally, dietary enquiry tools specifically designed for adolescents are direly needed. They should encompass household food security, food diversity (as indicator of nutritional quality), eating practices and underlying influences, and physical activity.
