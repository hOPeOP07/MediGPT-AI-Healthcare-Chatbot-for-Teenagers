It is seen in the proposed strategic model for addressing nutrition needs of adolescents (Fig. 2) that nutrition promotion not only includes healthy eating, but also the promotion of breastfeeding, of physical activity and of self-esteem. There is now sufficient evidence of the link between lack of self-esteem or sense of worth and distortions of body image and unhealthy eating practices to warrant more emphasis on strengthening self-esteem in adolescents, particularly in girls.
