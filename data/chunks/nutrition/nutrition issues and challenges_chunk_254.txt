Binge eating and night eating syndrome are eating disorders that are primarily found among obese persons. Although there is no clear evidence that these psychological conditions are the primary cause of people becoming obese, they are connected with the obese condition, since these and other eating disorders are as yet rare in cultures where obesity is not a social stigma (WHO 1998a). In modern societies where thinness is rather consistently emphasised, concerns about body weight are increasing and becoming evident at increasingly young age, particularly among girls (Dennison and Shepherd, 1995; Fox et al, 1994).
