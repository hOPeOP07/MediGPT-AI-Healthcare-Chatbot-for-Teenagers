The questionable reliability of self-reports on diet in the distant past, going back to adolescence, may also be an issue (Wolk et al, 1997). Many ecological, case-control and cohort studies provide convincing evidence of a preventive role of fruits and vegetables, including studies on the Mediterranean diet (Tavani and La Vecchia, 1995). Green leafy vegetables were found to be strongly associated with reduced ovarian cancer risk (Kushi et al, 1999).
