Indeed, adolescents are possibly less vulnerable to infection than they were at a younger age. This may contribute to their being somewhat neglected, but also it may mean that there is at adolescence less interference with adequate physiological utilization of food nutrients. Once final height is attained, stunting becomes a permanent consequence of past malnutrition rather than being a sign of present malnutrition.
