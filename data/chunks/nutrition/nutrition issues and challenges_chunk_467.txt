In developing countries, poorer women, adolescent or adult, are more likely to be undernourished, and food supplementation during pregnancy is seen as a potentially effective intervention. Metanalyses of data from controlled trials (Kramer 1993; Gülmezoglu, de Onis and Villar, 1998) revealed improvements, however modest, of foetal growth and birth weights with balanced energy/protein supplements.
