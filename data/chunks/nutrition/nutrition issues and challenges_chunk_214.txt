Since young age is implicated in the poor health consequences, the incidence of such consequences could be reduced if pregnancy were to be postponed into the mother’s adult years, or at least until 18. In populations where undernutrition is widespread, maturation is also delayed. It has been suggested that if the risks of overlapping growth with pregnancy are to be avoided, the first pregnancy should be postponed until girls are at least 19 years of age (Kurz, Peplinski and Johnson-Welch, 1994).
