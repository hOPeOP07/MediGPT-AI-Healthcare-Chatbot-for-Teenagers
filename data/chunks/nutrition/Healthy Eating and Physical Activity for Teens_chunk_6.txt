Life of a Teenager Description: This worksheet provides information on two hypothetical teenagers and their food choices and physical activity levels for one day. Title: Healthier Food Choices Description: Students will use this worksheet to create a more nutritious daily menu for one of the hypothetical cases. Title: Healthier Physical Activity Choices Description: Students will use this worksheet to create a healthier physical activity program for 5 days for one of the hypothetical cases. Title: Rubric for Scoring Student Posters Description: This document is used to assess student posters. It can also be used as a guide for students as they develop their posters.
