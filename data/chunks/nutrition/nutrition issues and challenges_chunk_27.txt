Based on a review of international surveys of youth for the Lisbon Conference of 1998, Richard and Braungart1 support this view, and contend that young people in general do not regard themselves as alienated, rebellious or antagonistic towards their families and adults. The majority of young people express positive views about themselves and their life situation, although a growing number do admit to some risky, experimental behaviours. Only a minority are involved in serious problems such as drug abuse, gangs and crime, and teenage pregnancy.
