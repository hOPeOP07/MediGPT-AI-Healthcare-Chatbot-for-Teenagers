In Australian adolescents, inadequate consumption of fruits, vegetables and dairy products was also observed (Nowak and Speare, 1996). In the USA, it was found that adolescents frequently had intakes of vitamin A, vitamin E, calcium, magnesium and zinc below recommended levels (Johnson et al, 1994). Females also had low intake levels of phosphorus and iron. At the same time, diets were high in some nutrients related to increased risk of chronic disease (total fat, saturated fat, sodium).
