The period of gradual transition from childhood to adulthood that normally begins with the onset of signs of puberty, is characterized by important psychological and social changes, not only physiological change. It is difficult to define in universal terms for there are important cultural differences. Depending on societies, the transition may be quick and the very notion of adolescence does not really exist, for instance where girls marry early and do not go to school.
