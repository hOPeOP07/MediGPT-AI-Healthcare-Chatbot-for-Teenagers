Thus, iodine deficiency, because of its lasting effects on survival and intellectual achievements, is a major obstacle to social and economic development. Iodine deficiency affects all age groups, but goitre primarily affects people aged 15-45 years, in particular women, as shown for example in Côte d’Ivoire (Kouame et al, 1998). There are rare instances of endemic goitre in the absence of iodine deficiency, like in one province of South Africa (Jooste et al, 1999).
