Using the 5th percentile of NCHS/WHO BMI reference values as an indicator of acute undernutrition, a report on Somali and Sudanese refugees in Kenya indicated that roughly 60% of 10-19 year-old people were undernourished9. However, the authors themselves suggest that this cut-off may overestimate the real extent of severe malnutrition in this age group, based on ancillary data on morbidity and mortality rates.
