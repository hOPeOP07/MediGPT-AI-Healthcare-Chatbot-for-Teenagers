Much research on various factors of nutritional risk in adolescents has been carried out in high-income countries, with many findings also of relevance to middle- and low-income ones. Therefore, the discussion paper deals with adolescent nutritional issues and programmes within their own setting. An important caveat is that because of very wide differences that may exist between and even within countries, generalizing problems or solutions is irrelevant, and the paper may be perceived as being over-simplistic in this regard because of the need to synthesize.
