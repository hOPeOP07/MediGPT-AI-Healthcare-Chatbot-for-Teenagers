We obtained, by and large, similar results in a pilot programme among Native Indian schoolchildren in Quebec designed for the same purpose (Delisle, Mavrikakis and Strychar, 1995). In Oslo, an integrated health education programme was introduced in schools for students aged 10-15 years in the early 80s, and the evaluation involved comparison with control schools (in WHO, 1993). The focus was nutrition, tobacco, alcohol and physical activity. Positive results were shown: fewer students started smoking, particularly among the younger ones.
