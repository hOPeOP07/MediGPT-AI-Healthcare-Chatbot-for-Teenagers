In large prospectice studies, total fat was not shown to be associated with breast cancer, and it may not be a risk factor for colon and prostate cancer either, contrary to correlations found among countries between dietary fat and these cancers (Willett 1998). High red meat consumption may be associated with colon cancer independently from fat intake, and animal fat (but not vegetable fat) appears to be related to risk of prostate cancer. Excess body fat appears nonetheless to be an important factor of post- menopausal breast cancer and possibly colon cancer.
