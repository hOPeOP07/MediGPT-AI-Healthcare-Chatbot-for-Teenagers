Girls were more likely to be dissatisfied with their body image and to diet, but also, to have better food habits than boys. Adolescent boys were more likely to exercise, and to be satisfied with their weight. Some racial and gender differences were confirmed in this study. Caucasian adolescents had the best diet quality score. Caucasian females were most likely to perceive themselves as overweight and to attempt weight loss, while males (especially those of African American backgrounds) were more likely to want to gain weight.
