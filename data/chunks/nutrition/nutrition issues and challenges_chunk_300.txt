There may be cultural differences, however, and it has been suggested to develop culture-specific tools for assessment of adolescents’ well-being, using a participatory approach, as part of empowering health promotion strategies (Joliot and Deschamps, 1997). Empowerment is a central element of the health promotion paradigm as defined in the Ottawa Charter (WHO 1986b). Empowerment is about increasing control over determinants of health.
