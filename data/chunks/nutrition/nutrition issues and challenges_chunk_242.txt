While recognizing that they often have poor food habits, American adolescents typically cite the lack of time, discipline, and a sense of urgency as barriers to altering their dietary behaviours (Story and Resnick, 1986). The relative influence of parents and peers may vary across cultures and sub-cultures, but a general trend is for social and external factors in general, and peers in particular, to become increasingly significant with transition to adolescence (Contento et al, 1988).
