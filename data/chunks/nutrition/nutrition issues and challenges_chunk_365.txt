For there is more than one dietary pattern that is consistent with health. WHO and FAO convened an international expert consultation on this issue in 1995 (WHO 1996b) and recently issued recommendations for developing such guidelines (WHO 1999b). Based on current scientific knowledge on nutritional requirements and on the types of diet-related diseases that are prevalent in a given population, dietary guidelines express the principles of good dietary practices (Latham 1997).
