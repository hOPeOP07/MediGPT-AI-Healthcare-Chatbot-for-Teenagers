However, it may not be so, unless nutrition concerns and objectives are explicit in health promotion programmes. Furthermore, nutrition promotion goes beyond nutrition education, defined as any set of learning experiences designed to facilitate the voluntary adoption of eating and other nutrition-related behaviours conducive to health and well-being (Bedworth and Bedworth, 1992).
