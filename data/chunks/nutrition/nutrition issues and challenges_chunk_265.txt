Studies have reported that the average American college student fails to meet the current physical activity recommendations of the American College of Sports Medicine, which involve at least three times per week of vigorous activities for cardiorespiratory endurance, a minimum frequency of three times a week for flexibility exercises, and twice a week for strength/endurance activities (Dinger and Waigandt, 1997). Vigorous activity was reported by 45% and moderate activity by 46% of respondents. While females engaged in flexibility and moderate physical activity more frequently than males, the reverse was true for vigorous exercise and strength/endurance activities.
