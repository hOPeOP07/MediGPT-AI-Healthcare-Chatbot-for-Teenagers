Such interventions do not single out risk populations and, therefore, reduce labeling and promote bonding to school and community. Community prevention programs that combine two or more effective programs, such as family- based and school-based programs, can be more effective than a single program alone. Community prevention programs reaching populations in multiple settings—for example, schools, clubs, faith-based organizations, and the media—are most effective when they present consistent, community-wide messages in each setting.
