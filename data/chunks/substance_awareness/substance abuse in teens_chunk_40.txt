On the other hand, families can serve a protective function when there is: • a strong bond between children and their families; • parental involvement in a child’s life; • supportive parenting that meets financial, emotional, cognitive, and social needs; and • clear limits and consistent enforcement of discipline. Finally, critical or sensitive periods in development may heighten the importance of risk or protective factors.
