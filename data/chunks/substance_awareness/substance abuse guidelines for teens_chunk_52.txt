Prevention will be the most effective when everyone becomes involved in keeping alcohol, tobacco, and other drug problems from happening. Drug use in this paper is measured by the percentage of a population using drugs during the last 30 days as measured by the National Household Survey on Drug Abuse. By reviewing this percentage, we can examine the effects of prevention over time even if the population decreases or increases because we are looking at the percent who have used or are using drugs.
