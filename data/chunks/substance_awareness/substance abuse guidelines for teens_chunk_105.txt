However, less than 10 percent of this same sample of high school seniors reported they would use the readily available marijuana in any given month. Where interdiction is contributing a great deal to prevention is when youth perceive that the perception of risk includes the chance of "being caught." For every high school senior who smoked marijuana in a given month, there were seven other seniors who did not use, although they said the drug was readily available.
