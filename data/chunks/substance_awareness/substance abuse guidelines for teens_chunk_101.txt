Toward the end of the 19th century, cocaine use increased dramatically because cocaine was vigorously marketed in a variety of forms, including as a "wonder drug" with me- dicinal qualities. Use decreased after the Harrison Act, which controlled cocaine, was enforced. The use of amphetamines increased in the 1950s due to increased availability and declined in the late 1960s and 1970s as people came to understand that these "uppers" were addictive and dangerous.
