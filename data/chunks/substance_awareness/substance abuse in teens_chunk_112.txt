Keeping records of content delivered, session attendance, content feedback quizzes, and independent observations of implementation fidelity can help monitor the effectiveness of program implementation and provide key information on why a program is or is not achieving its intended effects. How can the community implement and sustain effective prevention programs? After considering risk and protective factors within the community and selecting and adapting prevention programs to address those risks, the community must begin to implement those programs.
