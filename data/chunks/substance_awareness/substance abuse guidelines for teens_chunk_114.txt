The Partnership for a Drug-Free America, one of the most extensive private prevention efforts, estimates that it receives $1 million a day in the value of contributed advertising services and media time and space for public service announcements.56 There is no sound scientific or economic way to measure these private contributions. But, in discussions with many leaders in the field, a reasonable estimate is that the States, localities, and nonprofit organizations spend (or contribute labor equivalents of) $2 for every $1 spent by the Federal Government.
