In many communities, coalitions formed during the community planning process remain involved in overseeing program implementation. They continue to review progress toward goals and objectives set out in the community plan. Responsibility for actual implementation, however, generally resides within the local public or private community-based organization in the educational, social service, or other local system To ensure effective implementation, research-based school and family programs often require extensive human and financial resources and a serious commitment to training and technical assistance.
