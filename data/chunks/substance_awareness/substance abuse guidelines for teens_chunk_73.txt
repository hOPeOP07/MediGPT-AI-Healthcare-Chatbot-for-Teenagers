Prevention efforts are more efficacious for those under the age of 25 since few initiate alcohol, tobacco, or illicit drug use after this age (with some exceptions, e.g., cocaine, prescription drugs), and it is easier to shape the attitudes and influence the behavior of someone who has not started to use drugs than to change the behavior of those who already use/misuse. In addition, the age of first use is very important. Those who begin to use alcohol, tobacco, and other drugs at early ages also develop more problems associated with these substances.
