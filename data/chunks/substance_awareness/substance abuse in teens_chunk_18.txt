Prevention programs should be tailored to address risks specific to population or audience characteristics, such as age, gender, and ethnicity, to improve program effectiveness (Oetting et al. 1997). Preventing Drug Use among Children and Adolescents National Institute on Drug Abuse should enhance family bonding and relationships and include parenting skills; practice in developing, discussing, and enforcing family policies on substance abuse; and training in drug education and information (Ashery et al. 1998). Family bonding is the bedrock of the relationship between parents and children.
