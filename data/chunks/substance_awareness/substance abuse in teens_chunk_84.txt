The goal of these programs is to build new and strengthen existing protective factors and reverse or reduce modifiable risk factors in youth. Prevention programs can be described by the audience or intervention level for which they are designed: • Universal programs are designed for the general population, such as all students in a school. • Selective programs target groups at risk, or subsets of the general population such as children of drug abusers or poor school achievers. • Indicated programs are designed for people who are already experimenting with drugs.
