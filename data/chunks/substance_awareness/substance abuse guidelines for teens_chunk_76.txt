Drug use rates are higher for males than females in all age groups, for all drugs, and within ethnic and regional subgroups.32 Despite this disparity, prevention has worked for both males and females; the decade-long use trends for both sexes are down.33 The patterns are more complex for ethnic subgroups and are not as amenable to generalizations. Usage patterns vary by subgroup and drug. The National Household Survey obtains relatively small samples of specific Hispanic subgroups, American Indians, and AsianlPacific Islanders.
