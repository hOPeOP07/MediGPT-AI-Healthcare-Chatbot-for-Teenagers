For this reason, factors that affect early development in the family are probably the most crucial. Children are more likely to experience risk when there is: • lack of mutual attachment and nurturing by parents or caregivers; • a chaotic home environment; • lack of a significant relationship with a caring adult; and • a caregiver who abuses substances, suffers from mental illness, or engages in criminal behavior. These experiences, especially the abuse of drugs and other substances by parents and other caregivers, can impede bonding to the family and threaten feelings of security that children need for healthy development.
