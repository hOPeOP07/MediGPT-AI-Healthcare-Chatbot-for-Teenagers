The dramatic success of the Nation's alcohol, tobacco, and other drug problem prevention efforts could be easily misinterpreted. We cannot conclude that the problem is solved, and that additional prevention efforts are not needed. Millions of Americans are still at risk because they use alcohol, tobacco, or other drugs, and some are drug dependent.
