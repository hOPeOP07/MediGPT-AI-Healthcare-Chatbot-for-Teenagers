Further, few people start using drugs (especially alcohol and tobacco) past age 18, and additional delay in onset both protects individuals for the intervening years, as well as predicts fewer lifetime problems. Prevention programs aimed at those over the age of 25, such as drug-free workplaces, perinatal education programs for pregnant women, and well-baby clinics also have contributed greatly to decreasing the percentage of adults who use these substances.
