The Asian American Drug Abuse Program of South Central Los Angeles is a CSAP High Risk Youth Project that is a model collabora- tive effort between the Korean Youth Center and the Search to Involve Philippine Americans. These three agencies provide ATOD programs and servir.es to high-risk Asian Pacific Islander youth in the area. This project was especially visible during the 1992 South Central Los Angeles 11otS, working closely with other local organiza- tions to stop the violence and restabilize the community.
