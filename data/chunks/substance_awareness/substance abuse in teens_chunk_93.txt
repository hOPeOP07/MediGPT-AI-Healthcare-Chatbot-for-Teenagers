Research shows that the benefits from middle school prevention programs diminish without followup programs in high school. Prevention programs should include teacher training in good classroom management practices, such as rewarding appropriate student behavior. Such techniques help to foster student’s positive behavior, achievement, academic motivation, and school bonding. Prevention programs are most effective when they employ interactive techniques, such as peer discussion groups
