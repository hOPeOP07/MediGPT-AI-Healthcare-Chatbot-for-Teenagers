The Western Cape Government aims to change this by focusing on interventions that make a tangible impact on reducing the harms related to alcohol. Since 2016, the Western Cape Government has initiated several GameChanger programmes in three areas in the province, including the Alcohol Harm Reduction GameChanger. Strategies have been implemented to reduce access to alcohol, such as closing down illegal alcohol outlets, installing surveillance cameras and increasing law enforcement presence.
