It is also crucial to recognize and mitigate mental health issues early, and identify risk factors for future mental illness – including violence, neglect, abuse, school drop-out, poverty and parental mental illness.(13) Why is there underinvestment in adolescent mental health throughout the world? Although the overall burden of disease is lowest in young adolescents (aged 10-14 years), such data mask the importance of risk factors that are adopted or consolidated during adolescence but have their health impacts later in life.(9) Taken together with the relative lack of public health attention to mental health, adolescent mental health has not received the investment that it deserves, despite its enormous economic, social and personal burden.
