The preservation and promotion of mental health in the young population have conventionally been viewed as an individual or family responsibility; however, it is important to emphasize it at a much broader level. There is a need to promote the concept of positive health with public health approaches including expansion of the community services for mental disorders.[3,12] There is an urgent need to explore newer models of service delivery apart from standard models of hospital‑based care.
