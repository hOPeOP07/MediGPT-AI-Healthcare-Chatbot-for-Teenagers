Other lessons should emphasize the relation- ship between getting enough sleep and feel- ing well, and the potentially serious consequences of not getting enough sleep, such as falling asleep while driving. Adoles- cents need to understand that excessive moodiness, depression, irritability, fatigue, tiredness, aggressiveness and violence could be due to a sleep deficit. Measurable goals of a successful sleep education program include increased knowledge of how lack of sleep affects all aspects of an adolescent's life and expanded understanding and awareness of how to obtain more sleep.
