Finally, nurses need to do sleep research with adolescents to identify threats to the safety of adolescents who experience sleep problems and identify how schools, parents, and employers can help adolescents obtain sufficient sleep. An important role for school nurses is to educate the public and adolescents about adolescent sleep requirements.
