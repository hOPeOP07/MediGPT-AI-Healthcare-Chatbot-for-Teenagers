In addition to working with students, an important role for the school nurse is advis- ing, educating, and supporting parents about adolescent sleep requirements and suggesting ways to empower them to direct their ado- lescents' bedtimes and curfews so they obtain more sleep. School nurses need to inform business owners and work supervi- sors of the potential sleep deficits of their adolescent employees and recommend changing job activities that place adolescents at risk for injuries.
