Staying in bed, tossing and turning trying to get off to sleep can make us feel more stressed and keep us awake for longer. Having TVs, computers, and gadgets in your room helps you to sleep. FALSE. It’s best to not have any gadgets (including your phone) in your bedroom on a night.
