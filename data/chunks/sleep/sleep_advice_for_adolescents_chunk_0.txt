The following recommendations will help you get the best sleep possible and make it easier for you to fall asleep and stay asleep as well as to get enough sleep. n Sleep schedule: Wake up and go to bed at about the same time on school nights and non- school nights. Bedtime and wake time should not differ from one day to the next by more than an hour or so. Also set your bedtime so that you can get enough sleep. n Weekends: Don’t sleep in on weekends to “catch up” on sleep.
