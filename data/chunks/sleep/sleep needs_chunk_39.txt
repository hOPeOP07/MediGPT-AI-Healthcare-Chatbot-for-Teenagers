Nurses work- ing with adolescents can be effective coun- selors to investigate reasons why they do not sleep. They are in an influential position to design successful school educational pro- grams revolving around adolescent sleep needs. When adolescents come into the school nurse's office tired and wanting to sleep, explore possible reasons for this tired- ness and suggest solutions for change before letting them take a short nap.
