Endogenous rhythms, such as the sleep-awake and sleep-dream cycles, arise from within the individual. Circadian rhythms are a form of endogenous rhythm that reoccurs in a cyclic pattern ranging from 20 to 28 hours. Body temperature, blood pressure, pulse, respira- tions, urine production, and hormones (growth hormone is highest during night~ time sleeping), blood sugar, hemoglobin, and amino acid levels demonstrate a rhyth- mic pattern.
