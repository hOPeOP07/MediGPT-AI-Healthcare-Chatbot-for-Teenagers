For highly motivated and gifted high school students, the desire to excel and the excessive competition to be accepted into a prestigious college result in staying up late to complete homework and study for tests. Students who want to attend college and work part-time experience more sleep deprivation, since they may sacrifice sleep time to complete their assigned home- work or study for examinations.
