According to Carskadon (1989-1990), sleep patterns of students working 20 hours or more per week differ from those who do not work, or work fewer hours. Working students stay up later and sleep less on school nights. The students who worked extended hours reported more daytime sleepiness, falling asleep in class, fre- quent tardiness due to oversleeping, and greater use of caffeine, alcohol, and tobacco.
