These students had symptoms of day- time sleepiness and increased use of stimu- lants and alcohol. This study also found that students who worked long hours reported being sleepy while driving. Ten percent of high school males who worked 20 hours or more a week in addition to 20 hours per week in extracurricular activities admitted to having fallen asleep "at the wheel."
