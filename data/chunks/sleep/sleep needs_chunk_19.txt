REM sleep is called active or paradoxic sleep. REM occurs before the deeper sleep stages of rwo, three, and four and is charac- terized by rapid eye movements, intense brain activity, irregular respiration and heart rate, and dreaming. REM sleep occurs in 70- to 90-minute cycles that increase in length through the night. REM sleep rests and restores the brain and is important for learn- ing and psychological functioning.
