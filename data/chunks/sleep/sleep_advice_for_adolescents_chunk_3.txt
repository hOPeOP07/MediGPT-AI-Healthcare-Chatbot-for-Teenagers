Relaxing, calm, enjoyable activities, such as reading a book or listening to soothing music, help your body and mind slow down enough to let you sleep. Do not watch television, study, exercise, or get involved in “energizing” activities in the 30 minutes before bedtime. n Snack: Eat regular meals and don’t go to bed hungry. A light snack before bed is a good idea; eating a full meal in the hour before bed is not. n Caffeine: Avoid eating or drinking products containing caffeine in the late afternoon
