Biological rhythms are in repetitive and rhythmic patterns, and are found in plants, animals, and humans. The rhythms are found in the external and inter- nal environment and can be exogenous or endogenous. Exogenous rhythms depend on the rhythms of the external environment, such as seasonal variations, moon phases, and the day and night cycle.
