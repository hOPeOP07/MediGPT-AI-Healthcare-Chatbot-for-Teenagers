The time that school begins in the morning is influential on setting sleep pat- terns and the amount of sleep adolescents receive. In many school districts, junior high and high school students start school at an earlier time than elementary school students. This requires the older students to get up earlier to take a district school bus. The com- bination of late night activities, homework, employment, and early morning school attendance limits the hours available for sleep.
