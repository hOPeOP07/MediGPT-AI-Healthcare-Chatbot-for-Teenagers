For example, several studies have found that life events, such as interpersonal loss and legal or disciplinary problems have been linked to completed suicide (Jacobson and Gould, 2009). Childhood physical abuse (e.g., corporal punishment), sexual abuse, and neglect also have been related to suicidal behavior. The younger the person was at the time of the ﬁrst abuse, the higher the number of suicide attempts reported (Berk et al., 2010).
