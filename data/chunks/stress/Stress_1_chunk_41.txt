Additionally, an increase in distress is related with the increase in acne severity. Acne has a peak incidence during adolescence, a period typical for intensive preoccupation with body image and appearance. Therefore, besides the contribution of psychosocial stress to the onset of dermatological disease, the disease itself may result in signiﬁ- cant stress for the patient.
