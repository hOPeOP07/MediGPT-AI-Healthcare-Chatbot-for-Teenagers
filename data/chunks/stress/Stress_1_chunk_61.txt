Also, rates of completed as well as attempted suicide vary according to ethnicity. For example, the elevated rates of suicide among Native American youth are supposed to be associated with low social integration that results in limited access to various resources. On the other hand, lower rates of suicide among African American youth have been associated with their higher levels of religiosity compared with white youth.
