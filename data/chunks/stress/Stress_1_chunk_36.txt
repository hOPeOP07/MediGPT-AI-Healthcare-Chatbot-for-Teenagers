Research also shows that stressful life events have an impact on chronic illnesses in adolescents. Especially high incidence of stressful life events has been found in children and adolescents with cancer, who most frequently reported the experience of marital separation, death of a close family member, change in residence, and change in health of family members before the onset of cancer (Seiffge-Krenke, 1998).
