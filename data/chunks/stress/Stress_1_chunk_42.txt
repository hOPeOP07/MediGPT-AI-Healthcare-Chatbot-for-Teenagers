Several neurophysiological mechanisms have been impli- cated in stress-mediated skin disorders. Because stressful emotional experiences disturb the regulation of the HPA and the SAM systems, they may result in the alternations of glucocorticoid levels. For example, studies have found a relation between stress and an imbalance in the epidermal permeability barrier function, which is mediated by the increase in endogenous glucocorticoids.
