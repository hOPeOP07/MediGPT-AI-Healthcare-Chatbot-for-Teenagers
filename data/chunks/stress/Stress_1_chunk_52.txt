Furthermore, problem-focused coping has been found to correlate negatively with health problems and health risk behaviors, whereas avoidance coping correlated positively with these domains (Wilson et al., 2005). Generally, it could be concluded that when adolescents lack adequate coping skills, there is a risk from distress and suicidal attempts, smoking and substance use, and high-risk sexual behaviors (Rew, 2005). Social support is considered to be one of the most important psychosocial factors that inﬂuence the relationship between stress and health outcomes.
