Adolescents experience higher levels of stress who are from broken family or those who are going through parental divorce. 2.4 Identity Formation and Self-Esteem Individuals struggle with identity formation and issues related to self-esteem during this age. Concerns about one's own value, physical appearance, and future goals can lead to internal stress, particularly when combined with principles promoted by society and the media.
