Impact of Stress on Adolescents Adolescents face many changes in their social, mental, and physical life during this age. During this age stress might lead to major issues that endure a long time. Peer pressure, familial problems, educational pressures, or personal concerns are the main causes of stress in adolescents and it affect all aspects of an adolescent's life.
