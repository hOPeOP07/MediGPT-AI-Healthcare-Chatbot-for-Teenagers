Cross-Cultural Differences in the Effects of Stress on the Development of Adolescents As mentioned, developmental changes during adolescence, among other factors, also include adaptation to cultural expectations of becoming an adult. The greatest part of our knowledge about the effects of stress on development in adolescence was drawn from the research on the samples from Western societies. Theoretical assumptions as well as empirical evidence suggest the existence of cultural differences in the effects of stress on the adolescent development.
