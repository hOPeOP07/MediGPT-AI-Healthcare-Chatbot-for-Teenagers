In contrast, adolescents can predict the results of their actions by using logic to imagine multiple options and different situations. This new ability helps young people plan for their future and consider how their choices will affect that future. Another new skill adolescents develop is “thinking about thinking”— or metacognition. This practice enables youth to reflect on how they came to an answer or conclusion.
