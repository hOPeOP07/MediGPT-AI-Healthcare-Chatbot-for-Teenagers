They also learn to avoid the problems that strong emotions sometimes cause. However, because the brain’s frontal lobe— which is responsible for reasoning, planning, and problem-solving as well as emotions— doesn’t fully develop until the mid-twenties, adolescents may find it difficult to manage their emotions and think through the consequences of their actions.
