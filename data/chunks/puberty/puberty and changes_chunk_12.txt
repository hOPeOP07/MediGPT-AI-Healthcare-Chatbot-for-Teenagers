If there is somebody who smokes or drinks at home, they can become your teen’s role models. Poor self-esteem and the need to be ‘cool’ can push adolescents to smoke or drink. Easy access to substances like cigarettes, alcohol, drugs, and anabolic steroids may increase the temptation to try illicit substances. Keep an eye on your child’s behaviour.
