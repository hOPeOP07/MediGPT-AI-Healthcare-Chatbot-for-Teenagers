ENCOURAGE PARTICIPATION IN ACTIVITIES ADOLESCENTS CARE ABOUT Help adolescents make friends by getting them involved in activities that match their interests (e.g. art, music, computer science, sports). Show empathy by acting on concern for other people, using statements that describe how people might feel, and talking about being compassionate toward diverse groups of people. Adults and adolescents also can work together on community service projects.
