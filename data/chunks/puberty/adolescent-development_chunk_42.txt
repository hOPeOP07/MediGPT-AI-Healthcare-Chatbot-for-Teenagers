The stress response kicks in more quickly for adolescents than it does for adults whose brains are fully developed and can moderate a stress response. Not all stressors are bad. Positive experiences such as landing a first job or getting a driver’s permit can trigger a stress response that enables adolescents to approach a challenge with alertness and focus. Physical changes increase adolescents’ capacity for emotional awareness, self- management, and empathy, but emotional development is strongly influenced by context.
